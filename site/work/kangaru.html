Project 03 · Kangaru

A smarter way to power last-mile delivery

Kangaru is an EV-powered last-mile delivery platform that owns and operates its fleet. I designed the rider experience from onboarding to unlocking the first vehicle, and redesigned it mid-project when investment changed the business.

RoleProduct designer
Duration2 months · brief to final screens
TeamFounders, 1 developer, 1 designer (me)
StatusReal product · In development
Opportunity

Everyone built the vehicle. We built what runs on top.

Riders had no single view of earnings, no EV-specific charging guidance and limited visibility into vehicle health. EV adoption was growing fast, but attention was on hardware, so we saw room to own the software layer.

Pivot

The product had a ceiling

Software sold to fleet companies was limited by the fleet market, and a fleet could eventually build it in-house. When investment came in, Kangaru would own the fleet directly. The direction shifted, but the design problems stayed the same.

Research

Competitors, priorities and persona

Competitors charge riders even on bad days. We decided early: if a rider doesn't earn, we don't earn. No delivery, no fee.

Screens

Designing for riders who need to feel safe enough to act

Onboarding and KYC, hub homepage, vehicle selection, security deposit and booking confirmation.
